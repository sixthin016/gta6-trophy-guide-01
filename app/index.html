The save strategy (do this from minute one)

Make a manual save at the start of every chapter. The game has 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave, no mission replay and no New Game+. If you miss something, a chapter-start save is your only way back. Never overwrite your chapter-start saves with a single rolling slot: keep one save per chapter, labelled by chapter, until the platinum pops.

The road to the platinum — 6 stages, ~200 hours

Platinum difficulty 4/10. About 200 hours (PowerPyx) to 250+ hours (Push Square). One playthrough, 15 manual saves + 1 autosave. Cheats are all locked until after the story, and using one disables trophies, achievements and saving. No mission replay, no New Game+, no difficulty settings, no mission medals.
