      Save strategy — the rule that saves the run

      Make a manual save at the start of every chapter. The game has 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave, and no mission replay — an old chapter-start save is the only way back from a missed moment. Never spend all 15 slots on one chapter; keep one slot per chapter start, plus one spare before any one-off attempt (airport departure, jewelry store, showroom smash).

      Cheats disable trophies

      All 61 cheats stay locked until after the story. Using even one disables trophies, achievements and saving. Do not touch cheats until the platinum is earned.

      Method note — what this guide does and does not claim

      Names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags come from the official PlayStation 5 list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on every name, description and grade. Counts (250 stunts, 42 shooting-range challenges, 18 meditation spots) are PlayStation's own tracked targets. Guide text is written in our own words from those facts and the 9 sources above — a source is cited, never copied.

      Honesty note: 5 trophies are labelled UNCONFIRMED — In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin — because no launch-day method exists for them. This guide invents no steps for them. Personal Best carries a reported lead only (max Strength for both characters), not a confirmed method. Where sources disagree (meditation spots 18 vs 15, bank vaults 5 vs 8 vs 22 bank jobs, final mission Chapter 4 vs 5), both versions are shown.
