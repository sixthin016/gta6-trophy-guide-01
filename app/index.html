          Save strategy — your safety net

          1. Manual save at the start of every chapter — a fresh slot each time, never overwriting the chapter-start save.
          2. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 on the morning after Down to Earth, before the chapter ends.
          3. Finish every side-character strand before the finale — start each the moment its marker appears; race Yago when the hourglass shows in Chapter 3.
          4. Take Cal’s fishing challenge the moment he offers it — it may not come back. Save before every one-off attempt (showroom, plane, jewelry store, vaults).
          5. Do the partner trophies while Jason and Lucia are together — Hot Together and Retail Therapy need both of them.
